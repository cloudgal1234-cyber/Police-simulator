Patrol & Detective Simulator — prototype

You are Officer Reyes, on shift in a small city. Drive your cruiser to dispatch calls, step out at crime scenes, inspect evidence (◆ yellow markers), question witnesses (◆ blue markers) and accuse the culprit once you have enough evidence. A few suspects won't come quietly — those cases end in a short, tactical standoff.
